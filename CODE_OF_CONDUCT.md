# ph-surfaces community code of conduct

`ph-surfaces` welcomes careful, evidence-driven participation from people of
all backgrounds and experience levels. We want project spaces to be safe,
constructive, and focused on making embedded software more truthful.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas, code, evidence, and claims rather than people.
- State uncertainty and distinguish tests, models, code reading, and hardware
  observations.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's contact details,
  credentials, confidential material, or vulnerability information.
- Accept maintainer decisions about repository scope while remaining free to
  propose a documented reconsideration.
- Help make participation accessible by explaining assumptions and avoiding
  dismissive or exclusionary language.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, doxxing, and publishing private
communications without permission are not acceptable. Neither is using a
technical disagreement to target or belittle a contributor.

This policy applies in issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, release
coordination, and other spaces where someone is representing this project or
the Photon Circus organization.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not place sensitive conduct details in a public issue. To request a private
reporting channel, use this
[prefilled minimal-contact request](https://github.com/photon-circus/ph-surfaces/issues/new?title=Private%20conduct%20contact%20requested&body=Private%20conduct%20contact%20requested.)
without adding details; it reveals only that private contact is requested. A
maintainer will arrange a private channel. If the report concerns a repository
maintainer, use the same minimal wording in the
[organization standards repository](https://github.com/photon-circus/.github/issues/new?title=Private%20conduct%20contact%20requested&body=Private%20conduct%20contact%20requested.)
and ask for an organization owner without a conflict. For behavior governed by
GitHub's platform rules, you may also use GitHub's abuse-reporting tools.

Maintainers will review reports as privately and promptly as practical, avoid
conflicts of interest, and may edit or remove content, issue a warning, limit
participation, temporarily suspend participation, or permanently ban a person
from project spaces. Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith
concern is itself a violation.

Bad-faith or knowingly false reports may be handled under the same graduated
process. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately,
and shared publicly only to the extent needed to protect participants and the
project.
